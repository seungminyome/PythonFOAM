PythonFOAM: a vision-language observer for OpenFOAM runs

An optional observer for the PODFoam example of PythonFOAM. During an OpenFOAM run the solver hands its state to Python, which draws |U| and p and asks a vision-language model served locally by llama.cpp to describe the pictures, while the solver waits or keeps stepping. The answers are recorded; nothing from the model goes back into the solver.

The two pictures the running solver sent to Gemma 4 12B at step 250 of a developed backward-facing-step flow (t = 0.10125 s). These are v2 pictures: one field per picture, axes in percent of the plotted range, a grid line every 10 %. In a sequence, v2own gives each picture its own colour scale. The solver's default (v1) draws |U| and p in two panels with axes in metres.

A position counts as right when it lies within 10 % of the plotted range of the true cell. The constant baseline is the score of the best single answer given to every picture.

Gemma 4 E2B, the model of the CPU setup, does not give usable answers. Gemma 4 12B misreads most peak-speed changes under 2 %. No model warned of a coming crash more than 5 steps ahead, and simple numbers from the arrays do as well or better. Tested on 2D laminar flows and one extruded 3D case, on 2 MPI ranks without a model request, and with up to two requests in flight.
