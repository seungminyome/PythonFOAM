## Setup

Serial PODFoam, 20,540 cells, ten steps of 1e-7 s. After step 5 the callback
summarised the step (residuals, Courant and continuity numbers, an SVD summary
of the five snapshots so far, field ranges and domain extents) and rendered a
640x360 PNG with |U| above p, one polygon per cell, stretched vertically. The
request went to the local server; the solver waited for the reply, stored it
and ran steps 6 to 10.

| Run | Model | Steps | Files equal to baseline | Callback time |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| No request (baseline) | off | 10 | - | 0.755 s |
| Numbers + image | reply stored | 10 | 45 / 45 | 70.604 s |
| Numbers only | reply stored | 10 | 45 / 45 | 41.412 s |
| Connection refused | error stored | 10 | 45 / 45 | 0.411 s |

HTTP time was 70.149 s for the image request and 41.371 s for numbers only.
Rendering took 0.419 s. The server peaked at about 2.2 GiB PSS; the solver and
Python side at about 236 MiB RSS. The model was already loaded, so start-up is
not in these numbers.

## What the model said

With the image:

> The velocity magnitude |U| shows a high value near x=0.25 m, with a maximum of 82.5 m/s.

It went on to call this a high-speed region in the "central x-domain" with a
strong pressure gradient next to it.

Checked against the fields:

| | |
|---|---|
| Maximum \|U\| | 82.5275 m/s, cell 12090, centre (0.000538, 0.012714) m |
| Minimum p | -9362.83 m²/s², cell 10409, centre (0.000539, 0.012685) m |
| Model's x = 0.25 m vs true x | off by 0.249 m |

The 82.5 m/s was in the numbers it was given. The location was not, and the
answer is wrong: the maximum sits about 72% of the way across the plotted
x-range, not in the middle. The two extrema are in neighbouring cells, which is
consistent with a steep gradient but does not measure one.

The numbers-only reply quoted the pressure residual (9.19e-7) and mean Courant
number (3.4e-4) correctly and then suggested checking two things the input had
already stated: that the residuals are not a force balance, and that the SVD
was not centred.

## Why this run is not the comparison

The image request and the numbers-only request had different instructions and
the image request carried extra metadata. The difference between their answers
therefore says nothing about the image by itself. The
[replay runner](../Solver_Examples/PODFoam/llm_demo/README.md#replaying-a-saved-state-with-and-without-the-image)
was written after this run to fix that, and the October 17 report has its
results.

The SVD fractions sent (0.99997 for Ux, 0.99991 for Uy, 0.99999 for Uz) are
σ₁²/Σσᵢ² of the uncentred five-snapshot matrices. They describe how much of
the snapshot matrix the first mode carries. They are not a physical energy
share, and the Uz values are tiny to begin with.
