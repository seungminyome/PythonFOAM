# October 20 tests: asynchronous requests, describing one snapshot, describing a sequence

Code `9449085` · runs `pod-llm-async.pp7agC` (five asynchronous cases), `pod-llm-async.8YC6bG` (windowed frames), replays `pod-replay-20261020` · Gemma 4 E2B Q4_0 with image projector, llama.cpp b11429 on CPU

[README](../README.md) · [Evidence](evidence/2026-10-20/README.md) · [October 17 run](results.md) · [How to run](../Solver_Examples/PODFoam/llm_demo/README.md)

Three things were added after the October 17 report: the request can run
asynchronously, the model can be asked to describe one snapshot, and it can be
given several snapshots and asked how the flow changes. This report gives the
measurements for each. Nothing the model says goes back into the solver.

## Asynchronous requests inside the solver

Serial PODFoam on OpenFOAM v2106, 20,540 cells, 3000 steps of 5e-7 s
(t = 0 to 1.5e-3 s), fields and SVD modes written every 250 steps. The
request step forks the worker and returns; every later step checks the
worker's pipe without blocking and records the reply when it arrives.

| Run | Request at step | Reply recorded at step | Solver wait at the request step | Model wait | Files equal to baseline | Run time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| No request (baseline, frames rendered) | 1250 | 1261 | 0.004 s | - | - | 150.6 s |
| Describe one image | 250 | 1093 | 0.006 s | 34.0 s | 216 / 216 | 138.0 s |
| Sequence of three images | 1250 | 2321 | 0.004 s | 43.4 s | 216 / 216 | 136.5 s |
| Connection refused | 250 | 280 | 0.004 s | 0.002 s | 216 / 216 | 143.0 s |
| Server made to stall (4 s deadline) | 250 | 353 | 0.004 s | killed at 4.0 s | 216 / 216 | 147.2 s |

"Solver wait at the request step" is the C++-measured callback time at that
step: a fork, nothing else. Every other step's callback took under 0.005 s,
the last step 0.1 s (telemetry write). The reply to the single image arrived
843 steps after it was requested, the reply to the three images 1071 steps
after; both before the end of the run, so the solver never waited for the
model. The refused connection and the killed worker were recorded 30 and 103
steps after their request step. The 216 files are U, p, phi and the 15 SVD
mode fields at each of the 12 written times.

Run times are wall-clock for the whole solver process. The baseline is the
slowest; the differences between runs are machine noise, not the model. The
model wait is shorter than on October 17 (34 s against 78 s for one image)
because the system prompts were already in the server's prompt cache from a
shorter trial run an hour earlier (266 and 273 cached tokens); the image
tokens themselves were processed fresh (prompt processing 17.5 s for 570
tokens with one image, 32.4 s for 907 tokens with three).

### What the model said during the run

Describe, step 250, whole domain (x from -1.65 to 0.64 m, y from 0 to 0.114 m):

> The velocity magnitude |U| is relatively uniform across the domain, with a slight increase towards the right side. The kinematic pressure p is generally high in the lower-left and lower-right regions, and lowest near the center-right.

Fastest flow claimed at 90 % of x, 10 % of y; true cell at 72 %, 11 % (the
step corner, x = 0.0005 m). Lowest pressure claimed at 50 %, 10 %; true at
72 %, 11 %.

Sequence, steps 250, 750, 1250:

> The velocity magnitude |U| shows a localized increase in the central region over time, and the kinematic pressure p also shows a corresponding change, with the pressure minimum becoming more negative.

Trend words: max speed `increasing`, min pressure `more_negative`, fastest
spot `same_place`. From the arrays: max |U| 60.2, 49.7, 46.7 m/s
(decreasing), min p -1467, -897, -792 m²/s² (less negative), spot in the same
cell. One of three right.

## Replays of saved frames

The same prompts were then sent again from saved frames, three repeats each
(temperature 0), so that the answers can be scored against the arrays. Two
views were used: the whole domain (x from -1.65 to 0.64 m) and a window
around the step (x from -0.05 to 0.15 m, `LLM_IMAGE_XRANGE`). In the window
the step corner, the fast spot and the recirculation behind the step fill
the picture; in the whole-domain view they are a few pixels wide.

### One snapshot: where are the fastest flow and the lowest pressure?

The model answers positions as percent of the plotted x range from the left
and of the plotted y range from the bottom. The true cell is the maximum of
|U| (minimum of p) inside the plotted window.

| View | Step | Fastest flow, answer | True | Error | Lowest pressure, error |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|---:|
| Whole domain | 250 | (90, 10) | (72, 11) | 17.9 % of range, 0.39 m | 22.1 % |
| Whole domain | 750 | (85, 5) | (72, 11) | 14.2 %, 0.28 m | 62.3 % |
| Whole domain | 1250 | (85, 5) | (72, 11) | 14.1 %, 0.27 m | 87.9 % |
| Window | 250 | (10, 90) | (25, 11) | 80.3 %, 0.095 m | 15.3 % |
| Window | 1250 | (75, 50) | (27, 11) | 61.4 %, 0.105 m | 63.1 % |
| Window | 2750 | (80, 50) and (80, 5) | (30, 11) | 63.3 % and 50.4 %, 0.11 and 0.10 m | 19.9 % and 21.4 % |

Eighteen answers, none within 10 % of the plotted range of the true fast
spot. In the whole-domain view every answer puts the fastest flow at the far
right (85 to 90 %); the true spot is at 72 %, the step corner, and the
descriptions call the field "relatively uniform" with "a slight increase
towards the right side". In the window the corner fills the picture and the
answers are further off in percent terms (upper left, centre right, upper
right). The three repeats gave the same answer in five of the six frames; at
step 2750 in the window the first repeat differed from the other two (the
server's prompt cache was in a different state, see the cached token counts
in the records).

### Three snapshots: how does the flow change?

| View | Frames | Max \|U\| from the arrays | Answer | Min p from the arrays | Answer | Fast spot | Answer | Right |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---:|
| Whole domain | 250, 750, 1250 | 60.2 to 46.7 m/s, decreasing | increasing | -1467 to -792, less negative | more_negative | same place | same_place | 1 of 3 |
| Window | 250, 1250, 2750 | 60.2 to 42.3 m/s, decreasing | increasing | -1467 to -622, less negative | less_negative | same place (moved 4.7 % of the range) | same_place | 2 of 3 |

All three repeats of each gave the same words. Both views got the speed
trend backwards although the colour-bar maxima fall from about 60 to 47 (or
42). The whole-domain descriptions speak of "a localized increase in the
central region"; the window descriptions say the peak value "increases ...
and spreads over time". `same_place` was right both times, but it is also
the answer that needs the least from the pictures.

### Cost

Replay latencies depend on the server's prompt cache. A describe request with
only the system prompt cached took 26 to 33 s; a repeat of the same request
15 to 18 s. The three-image sequence request took 45 s with little cached and
11 to 12 s when the whole prompt was cached. Prompt sizes: 584 tokens for one
image, 880 to 907 for three (about 270 tokens per image at the 256-token
image setting). These numbers are for this CPU and do not separate the cost
of the image from the cost of the text.

## What this shows, and what it does not

Works: the solver no longer waits for the model. A request is a fork at one
step and a non-blocking check at every later step; replies, refused
connections and killed workers are all recorded with the step at which they
were noticed, and the written fields are byte-identical to a run without
requests.

Not shown: that Gemma 4 E2B reads these pictures. On one snapshot it never
placed the fastest flow within 10 % of the plotted range of the true spot
(18 answers, two views, three states). On three snapshots it called the peak
speed increasing in both views while the colour bars show it falling, and it
got two of the three trend words right only in the windowed view. The
positions and trends it gives are not usable as a description of this flow.
Whether a larger model does better is the open question; the scripts for the
12B model on a GPU node are unchanged and still untested.

Caveats: one case, one model, three states, three repeats at temperature 0
that mostly return the same text. Repeats of one request are not independent
cases, and a repeat with a warm prompt cache is not the same computation as
the first. The scoring is only as good as the plotted window: in the
whole-domain view the true spot is a few pixels, so a position error of 14 %
is partly a problem of the picture, which is why the window was added.

Not covered: MPI, 3D, more than one request in flight, the 12B model.
