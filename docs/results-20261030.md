# October 30: OpenFOAM versions, the original PODFoam and a wrong-setup test

MedicineBow cluster, 2026-10-30 · NVIDIA A30, NVIDIA L40S and CPU nodes · llama.cpp b11429, commit `d812350` · Slurm jobs and code: [Records](#records)

[README](../README.md) · Evidence: [versions and the original PODFoam](evidence/2026-10-30-versions/README.md), [wrong-setup test](evidence/2026-10-30-wrong-setup/README.md) · Later: [November 2](results-20261102.md), [November 3](results-20261103.md) · [Korean](https://github.com/seungminyome/PythonFOAM-Korean/blob/main/docs/results-20261030.md)

In short: the observer builds and passes its checks on OpenFOAM v2012, v2106, v2212
and 8. The original PODFoam's POD modes differ from this fork's because the first
snapshot of each SVD window was overwritten. On OpenFOAM 8 the flow fields of the two
codes are identical. In a first wrong-setup test Gemma 4 31B beat a simple rule, but the lead
came from two runs. The [November 3 report](results-20261103.md) tests this result again.

PODFoam with the observer builds and passes its bridge validation on OpenFOAM v2012,
v2106, v2212 and 8. On v2012, v2106 and v2212 the observer's frames are identical byte
for byte.

OpenFOAM 8 differs from v2212 by up to 3.18 m/s in U at step 100. The cause is that
OpenFOAM 8 applies the case's `relaxationFactors` with one outer corrector and
openfoam.com does not ([November 2](results-20261102.md#why-openfoam-8-differs-from-v2212)).

Against the original PODFoam on OpenFOAM 8, U, p and phi are byte-identical, and the
POD modes differ. At `6fde869` the original code kept the first snapshot of each SVD
window as a view of a C++ buffer, and the next snapshot overwrote that buffer. With the
overwrite reproduced in Python, every POD mode equals the original's.

On openfoam.com, U and p differ from the original's because the PR tree solves p as
that version's `pimpleFoam` does. At the first written time p differs by up to
847 m²/s², where the original's largest |p| is 5,142 m²/s². With the original pressure line and the overwrite reproduced in Python,
every POD mode equals the original's, and U, p and phi are byte-identical.

Asked whether a run was set up wrong, Gemma 4 31B was right for 67 of 80 frames. A
rule on the peak |U| was right for 56, and the lead of 31B rests on two runs.

The bridge validation runs ten serial steps of PODFoam against `pimpleFoam`. It checks
the snapshots Python holds, the POD modes it returns, and U and p
([its README](../Solver_Examples/PODFoam/validation/README.md)). With `LLM_ENABLED=0`
the observer draws frames and writes telemetry but sends no model request. A frame is
`U.png`, `p.png`, `fields.npz` and `readable-metadata.json` of one time step.

The original PODFoam is [argonne-lcf/PythonFOAM](https://github.com/argonne-lcf/PythonFOAM)
at `6fde869`. The PR tree is that code plus this fork's solver and observer files,
without the experiment scripts. A stand-in server gives a fixed answer and reads no
picture. Differences between two frames are the largest |ΔU| over the cells and the
largest |Δp| with each p's mean removed. They are given against the peak |U| and the
range of p.

## The original PODFoam

The original PODFoam and the PR tree ran the shipped `python_module.py` on two cases.
One is the shipped `Run_Case` with `endTime` 1.5×10⁻⁴ s and `writePrecision` 12. The
other is the validation's stabilised copy: laminar, upwind, Δt 1×10⁻⁷ s, 200 steps,
written every 20. Each module also saved its snapshot matrix at every SVD. The table
has the runs of the PR tree's final code, `bf7a06e`.

| Case, version, job | U, p, phi | POD modes equal to the original's | The same with the overwrite in Python | With also the original pressure line |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Shipped, 8, 24951136 | byte-identical, 2 of 2 written times | 0 of 30 | 30 of 30 | |
| Stabilised, 8, 24951137 | byte-identical, 10 of 10 | 0 of 150 | 150 of 150 | |
| Stabilised, v2012, 24951138 | differ, 10 of 10 | 0 of 150 | 0 of 150 | 150 of 150; U, p, phi byte-identical |
| Stabilised, v2106, 24951139 | differ, 10 of 10 | 0 of 150 | 0 of 150 | 150 of 150; U, p, phi byte-identical |
| Stabilised, v2212, 24951140 | differ, 10 of 10 | 0 of 150 | 0 of 150 | 150 of 150; U, p, phi byte-identical |

- **POD modes.** At `6fde869` the shipped module kept the first snapshot of a window
  as a view of the C++ buffer, and the next snapshot overwrote that buffer. So the
  first two columns of every snapshot matrix were equal: 6 of 6 matrices in the
  shipped run and 30 of 30 in the stabilised run on OpenFOAM 8.
- In the PR tree each snapshot is an array that NumPy owns, and the two columns
  differ. All other columns are equal on OpenFOAM 8.
- **Pressure on openfoam.com.** With one outer corrector, the original plain
  `pEqn.solve()` selects `pFinal` at every corrector there. That version sets its
  `finalIteration` flag once per outer iteration
  ([November 2](results-20261102.md#the-pressure-solver-on-openfoamcom)).
- The PR tree uses `pFinal` only on the last non-orthogonal pass of the final
  corrector, as that version's `pimpleFoam` does. The differences have the same size
  on all three versions:

| Written time | Largest difference in a component of U, m/s | Largest difference in p, m²/s² |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2×10⁻⁶ s | 0.0508 of 75.8 | 847 of 5,142 |
| 2×10⁻⁵ s | 0.0109 of 73.2 | 31.8 of 3,456 |

- **Shipped `Run_Case` on openfoam.com.** With the same `endTime` and `writePrecision`
  it diverges with both builds on v2106 (job 24951141). The `LESModel` line that job
  added changes nothing there, because v2012 and later read the case's `model` entry
  first.
- The original build exited 1 in step 89 on a `nan` read back from
  `data.solverPerformance.p`, and the PR tree exited 136 with SIGFPE in step 86. The
  sibling solvers diverged there too
  ([November 2](results-20261102.md#the-sibling-solvers)).

**POD schedule.** With `podSvdInterval` 0 and no `analysis_func`, the PR tree runs the
SVD at write steps, as the original code at `6fde869` did. One rule is new: a write
step with no snapshot since the last SVD collects one, where the original code ran
`svd_func` at every write step. In the table `c` is a snapshot, `V` an SVD instead of
a snapshot, and `S` a snapshot and an SVD.

| Module | `analysis_func` | `writeInterval` | `podSvdInterval` | The PR tree | The original build |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| shipped | no | 1 | 0 | `cVcVcVcVcVcV` | signal 11, no snapshot |
| shipped | no | 3 | 0 | `ccVccVccVccVccVccVcc` | exit 0, 14 snapshots, as here |
| shipped | no | 25 | 0 | 24 `c`, `V`, 24 `c`, `V`, 10 `c` | exit 0, 58 snapshots, as here |
| shipped | yes | 3 | 0 | `ccSccSccSccSccSccScc` | |
| shipped | no | 3 | 7 | `ccccccSccccccSccccccS` | |
| shipped | yes | 3 | 2 | `cScScScScScScScScScSc` | |

All 20 runs of the PR tree, serial and on 2 ranks, match a table predicted from the
stated rule. That holds on OpenFOAM 8 and v2106 (jobs 24950749 and 24950748) and on
v2012 and v2212 ([November 2](results-20261102.md#error-paths-and-the-pod-schedule-on-v2012-and-v2212)).

**What else changes for a PODFoam user.** The full list is in the
[validation README](../Solver_Examples/PODFoam/validation/README.md#what-changes-in-podfoam).

- Mode files are dimensionless `zeroGradient` fields. The original build wrote them as
  copies of U's components, with U's dimensions and calculated patches that kept U's
  boundary values.
- Floating-point traps are on in the solver and off around Python. A run that goes on
  through an overflow in the original build stops with SIGFPE here.
- `online_mode = False` in the shipped module stopped at `writeInterval` 3 and 6 in
  short tests. A tiny negative eigenvalue gives a NaN under `np.sqrt`, and the
  returned POD modes must be finite.
- The original build exited 0 on the same cases (jobs 24950748 and 24950749).
- `Py_FinalizeEx()` runs at the end and waits for non-daemon threads, so a module
  that starts one must also stop it. The test module with such a thread was stopped
  by its timeout here, and the original build exited 0.
- `podSvdInterval` above 0 can write a time folder that holds only POD fields. If that
  is the newest folder, `startFrom latestTime` stops on the missing `p`.

## Can a model see that a run was set up wrong?

Four models were shown the two v2 pictures of one state of a `pimpleFoam` run of the
step case. They were told the intended setup in words and asked whether the pictures
are consistent with it. No kind of mistake is named.

The set has 80 requests: 8 states each of five normal runs and of five runs with one
changed entry. Every run ended normally
([evidence](evidence/2026-10-30-wrong-setup/README.md)).

A frame is right when a normal frame is answered `consistent` or a wrong frame
`inconsistent`. `cannot_tell` is wrong on both, and no model answered it. The rule says
`consistent` when the peak |U| in the window lies between one and two times the stated
32.2 m/s.

| Run | Change | 12B | 26B-A4B | 31B | Qwen | Rule |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| N1 to N5 | none, 40 frames | 40 | 40 | 38 | 40 | 40 |
| M1 | inlet velocity 3.22 m/s instead of 32.2 m/s | 1 | 0 | 8 | 0 | 8 |
| M2 | ν = 1.56×10⁻³ m²/s instead of 1.56×10⁻⁵ m²/s | 0 | 2 | 8 | 5 | 0 |
| M3 | `lowerWall` and `upperWall` `slip` instead of `noSlip` | 0 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| M4 | `upperWall` `fixedValue (-32.2 0 0)` instead of `noSlip` | 0 | 0 | 4 | 0 | 0 |
| M5 | inlet faces in the lower half at velocity 0, 32 of 65; the others at 32.2 m/s | 0 | 0 | 8 | 0 | 8 |
| **Right of 80** | | **41** | **42** | **67** | **45** | **56** |

The constant baseline gets 40 of 80. Any rule on the request text also gets 40,
because the text differs only in the frame's step and time. Each of the 8 texts occurs
for 5 normal and 5 wrong frames.

- **31B against the rule.** They differ on 15 frames: 31B is right on 13, the rule on
  2. Without M2 and M4, 31B gets 55 and the rule 56 of 64.
- **M1 and M5.** All 16 reasons of 31B compare the colour bar's maximum with the
  stated speed, which is the rule's comparison.
- **M4.** The change is outside the plotted window, so M4 cannot show whether a model
  notices it. The 4 answers of 31B use the same words as its 2 false alarms on normal
  frames.
- **M2, the viscosity run,** is the only case that models notice and that no number
  without a normal run flags. It is weak: one run, whose frames look alike.
- The M2 frames show no vortices and a smooth p. 13 of the 15 reasons deny a
  recirculation that is there, and no reason names viscosity.

Checks outside the design rest on the records listed in
[what the counts rest on](evidence/2026-10-30-wrong-setup/README.md#what-the-counts-rest-on).

| Check | Needs a normal run | Right of 80 | False alarms of 40 |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Largest Courant number of the whole run above 1, which flags M1, M3 and M5 | no | 64 | 0 |
| Any of the four colour-bar end numbers outside the normal frames' range | yes | 75 | 0 |
| The same, each normal run against the other four runs' range | yes | 68 | 7 |

One AI agent judged the stated reasons, with no second judge and no human check. Of
the 37 `inconsistent` reasons on wrong frames, 14 name the change or its visible
effect, 17 partly and 6 do not.

## Limitations

- On v2012 and OpenFOAM 8 each version had one 12B request here, and the stand-in
  server read no picture. The 12B suite ran on both on November 1
  ([November 2](results-20261102.md#12b-suites-on-v2012-and-openfoam-8-and-the-6000-step-default)).
- This code ran under MPI here only with no model requests. A 2-rank run with one 12B
  request followed on November 1, and only rank 0 sends.
- No model read a slice picture here. The slice test and the instability test without
  stated times followed on November 1 and 2.
- The jobs here set 1,000 steps. The default 6,000 steps of `async_v2.slurm` ran on
  v2212 on November 1 and passed.
- `setup_12b.sh` ran here only with a stand-in `curl`. The real download followed on
  November 2, and the prebuilt CUDA binaries it unpacks do not run on this cluster's
  glibc 2.34.
- `setup_runtime.sh` in job 24950804 found the E2B files in place and downloaded
  nothing. `LLAMA_CUDA=13.4` was not run.
- A start state for `LLM_LONG_START` made by continuing a validation run's
  `fixed-pimple` was not tried with this code; the runs here used the developed state
  of October 22.
- At `6fde869`, `PODFoam_Debug`, `APMOSFoam` and `AEFoam` kept the first snapshot the
  same way. Their runs with the shipped module on November 1 and 2 showed the first two
  columns equal in every matrix
  ([November 2](results-20261102.md#the-sibling-solvers)).
- The wrong-setup test has one case and five wrong runs. Its normal runs are pieces
  of one trajectory, so other normal runs might lie outside its ranges.
