# October 30: OpenFOAM versions, upstream's PODFoam and a wrong-setup test

MedicineBow cluster, 2026-10-30 · NVIDIA A30, NVIDIA L40S and CPU nodes · llama.cpp b11429 (commit `d812350`) · Slurm jobs and code: [Records](#records)

[README](../README.md) · Evidence: [versions and upstream comparison](evidence/2026-10-30-versions/README.md), [wrong-setup test](evidence/2026-10-30-wrong-setup/README.md) · [Korean](https://github.com/seungminyome/PythonFOAM-Korean/blob/main/docs/results-20261030.md)

PODFoam with the observer builds and passes its bridge validation on OpenFOAM
v2012, v2106, v2212 and 8. On v2012, v2106 and v2212 the observer's frames are
identical byte for byte. OpenFOAM 8 differs from v2212 at step 100 by at most
3.11 of 37.2 m/s in U and 23.2 of 379 m²/s² in p; the cause was not examined.
Against upstream's PODFoam on OpenFOAM 8, U, p and phi are byte-identical. POD
modes differ, because upstream's first snapshot of each window is overwritten
by the next one; with that overwrite reproduced in Python, all modes equal
upstream's (30 of 30 and 150 of 150). On openfoam.com the same holds with
upstream's pressure line. This fork's line solves p as that version's
`pimpleFoam` does, which changes U by at most 0.0508 of 75.8 m/s and p by 847
of 5,142 m²/s² after 2×10⁻⁶ s. Asked whether a run was set up wrong, Gemma 4
31B was right for 67 of 80 frames and 12B, 26B-A4B and Qwen3.5-35B-A3B for 41,
42 and 45, against a constant baseline of 40 and 56 for a rule on the peak
|U|. Without the two runs that 31B's lead rests on, it gets 55 and the rule 56
of 64.

The bridge validation is ten serial steps of PODFoam against `pimpleFoam`: the
snapshots Python holds, the modes it returns and U and p
([its README](../Solver_Examples/PODFoam/validation/README.md)). The observer
with no model (`LLM_ENABLED=0`) draws frames and writes telemetry and sends no
request. A frame is `U.png`, `p.png`, `fields.npz` and
`readable-metadata.json` of one step. Upstream is
[argonne-lcf/PythonFOAM](https://github.com/argonne-lcf/PythonFOAM) at
`6fde869`. The PR tree is branch `pr/podfoam-observer`: upstream plus this fork's
solver and observer files, without the experiment scripts. A stand-in server
gives a fixed reply and reads no picture.
Differences between two frames are the largest |ΔU| over the cells and the
largest |Δp| with each p's mean removed, given against the peak |U| and the
range of p.

## Upstream's PODFoam

Upstream's and this fork's PODFoam ran upstream's `python_module.py` on two
cases: the shipped `Run_Case`, and the validation's stabilised copy (laminar,
upwind, Δt 1×10⁻⁷ s, 200 steps, written every 20). Each module also saved its
snapshot matrix at every SVD. The table has the runs of the PR tree's final
code, `bf7a06e`.

| Case, version (job) | U, p, phi | POD modes equal to upstream's | The same with the overwrite in Python | With also upstream's pressure line |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Shipped, 8 (24951136) | byte-identical, 2 of 2 written times | 0 of 30 | 30 of 30 | |
| Stabilised, 8 (24951137) | byte-identical, 10 of 10 | 0 of 150 | 150 of 150 | |
| Stabilised, v2012 (24951138) | differ, 10 of 10 | 0 of 150 | 0 of 150 | 150 of 150; U, p, phi byte-identical |
| Stabilised, v2106 (24951139) | differ, 10 of 10 | 0 of 150 | 0 of 150 | 150 of 150; U, p, phi byte-identical |
| Stabilised, v2212 (24951140) | differ, 10 of 10 | 0 of 150 | 0 of 150 | 150 of 150; U, p, phi byte-identical |

- **POD modes.** Upstream's module keeps the first snapshot of a window as a
  view of the C++ buffer, and the next snapshot overwrites that buffer. So the
  first two columns of every snapshot matrix are equal: 6 of 6 matrices (u, v
  and w at each SVD) in the shipped run and 30 of 30 in the stabilised run on
  OpenFOAM 8. In this fork each snapshot is an array that Python owns, and the
  two columns differ. All other columns are equal on OpenFOAM 8.
- **Pressure on openfoam.com.** With one outer corrector, upstream's plain
  `pEqn.solve()` selects `pFinal` at every corrector there. This fork uses the
  `p` solver until the final corrector, as that version's `pimpleFoam` does.
  The sizes are the same on all three versions:

| Written time | Largest difference in a component of U, m/s | Largest difference in p, m²/s² |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2×10⁻⁶ s | 0.0508 of 75.8 | 847 of 5,142 |
| 2×10⁻⁵ s | 0.0109 of 73.2 | 31.8 of 3,456 |

- **Shipped `Run_Case` on openfoam.com.** It needs `LESModel` added by hand, and
  then it diverges with both builds (v2106, job 24951141): upstream's exits 1
  after 90 steps on a `nan` read back from `solverPerform`, and this fork's
  exits 136 (SIGFPE) after 87 steps.

**POD schedule.** With `podSvdInterval` at 0, a module without `analysis_func`
keeps upstream's schedule. `c` is a snapshot, `V` an SVD instead of a snapshot,
`S` a snapshot and an SVD.

| Module | `analysis_func` | `writeInterval` | `podSvdInterval` | This fork | Upstream's build |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| upstream's | no | 1 | 0 | `cVcVcVcVcVcV` | signal 11, no snapshot |
| upstream's | no | 3 | 0 | `ccVccVccVccVccVccVcc` | exit 0, 14 snapshots, as here |
| upstream's | no | 25 | 0 | 24 `c`, `V`, 24 `c`, `V`, 10 `c` | exit 0, 58 snapshots, as here |
| upstream's | yes | 3 | 0 | `ccSccSccSccSccSccScc` | |
| upstream's | no | 3 | 7 | `ccccccSccccccSccccccS` | |
| upstream's | yes | 3 | 2 | `cScScScScScScScScScSc` | |

All 20 runs of this fork, 15 serial and 5 on 2 ranks, match a table predicted
from the stated rule on OpenFOAM 8 and v2106 (jobs 24950749 and 24950748).

**What else changes for a PODFoam user** (from the
[validation README](../Solver_Examples/PODFoam/validation/README.md#what-changes-from-upstreams-podfoam)):

- Mode files are dimensionless `zeroGradient` fields. Upstream's are copies of
  U's components, with U's dimensions and boundary conditions.
- Floating-point traps are on in the solver and off around Python, so a run
  that goes on through an overflow in upstream's build stops with SIGFPE here.
- `online_mode = False` in the shipped module stopped at `writeInterval` 3 and
  6 in short tests, because a tiny negative eigenvalue gives a NaN under
  `np.sqrt` and the returned modes must be finite. Upstream's build exited 0 on
  the same cases (jobs 24950748 and 24950749).
- `Py_FinalizeEx()` runs at the end and waits for non-daemon threads, so a
  module that starts one must also stop it. The test module with such a thread
  was stopped by its timeout here, and upstream's build exited 0.
- `podSvdInterval` above 0 can write a time folder holding only POD fields. If
  that is the newest folder, `startFrom latestTime` stops on the missing `p`.

## Can a model see that a run was set up wrong?

Four models were shown the two v2 pictures of one state of a `pimpleFoam` run
of the step case, were told the intended setup in words and were asked whether
the pictures are consistent with it. No kind of mistake is named. The set has
80 requests: 8 states each of five normal runs and of five runs with one changed
entry, and every run ended normally after 8,000 steps
([evidence](evidence/2026-10-30-wrong-setup/README.md)).

A frame is right when a normal frame is answered `consistent` or a wrong frame
`inconsistent`; `cannot_tell` is wrong on both, and no model answered it. The
rule says `consistent` when the peak |U| in the window lies between 1 and 2
times the stated 32.2 m/s.

| Run | Change | 12B | 26B-A4B | 31B | Qwen | Rule |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| N1 to N5 | none (40 frames) | 40 | 40 | 38 | 40 | 40 |
| M1 | inlet velocity 3.22 m/s instead of 32.2 m/s | 1 | 0 | 8 | 0 | 8 |
| M2 | ν = 1.56×10⁻³ m²/s instead of 1.56×10⁻⁵ m²/s | 0 | 2 | 8 | 5 | 0 |
| M3 | `lowerWall` and `upperWall` `slip` instead of `noSlip` | 0 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| M4 | `upperWall` `fixedValue (-32.2 0 0)` instead of `noSlip` | 0 | 0 | 4 | 0 | 0 |
| M5 | inlet faces in the lower half at velocity 0 (32 of 65), the others at 32.2 m/s | 0 | 0 | 8 | 0 | 8 |
| **Right of 80** | | **41** | **42** | **67** | **45** | **56** |

The constant baseline gets 40 of 80. Any rule on the request text also gets
40, because the text differs only in the frame's step and time, and each of
the 8 texts occurs for 5 normal and 5 wrong frames.

- **31B against the rule.** They differ on 15 frames: 31B is right on 13 (M2 8,
  M4 4, M3 1), the rule on 2. Without M2 and M4 it is 55 against 56 of 64.
- **M1 and M5.** All 16 of 31B's reasons compare the colour bar's maximum with
  the stated speed, which is the rule's comparison.
- **M4.** The change is outside the plotted window, so M4 cannot show whether a
  model notices it. 31B's 4 use the same words as its 2 false alarms on normal
  frames.
- **M2 (viscosity)** is the only candidate here that models notice and no number
  needing no reference run flags: 31B 8, Qwen 5, 26B-A4B 2, 12B 0. It is weak.
  It is one run, whose frames are nearly alike. The frames show no vortices and
  a smooth p, and 13 of the 15 reasons deny a recirculation that is there: Ux < 0
  in 43.0 to 45.1 % of the window's cells against 22.5 to 33.7 % in normal frames.
  No reason names viscosity.

Numbers outside the design, computed after the answers were read:

| Check | Needs a normal run | Right of 80 | False alarms of 40 |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Largest Courant number of the whole run above 1 (flags M1, M3, M5) | no | 64 | 0 |
| Any of the four colour-bar end numbers outside the normal frames' range | yes | 75 | 0 |
| The same, each normal run against the other four runs' range | yes | 68 | 7 |

The stated reasons were judged by one AI agent, with no second judge and no
human check. Of the 37 `inconsistent` reasons on wrong frames, 14 name the
change or its visible effect, 17 partly and 6 do not.

## Limitations

- No suite with a model ran on v2012 or OpenFOAM 8; there each version had one
  12B request, and the stand-in server read no picture.
- No model request ran in parallel: this code under MPI ran only with no model.
- No model has read a slice picture. The 3D slice reading and the instability
  test with equally spaced frames were not run.
- The default 6,000 steps of `async_v2.slurm` as it is now were not run; the
  jobs above set 1,000. `setup_12b.sh` ran only with a stand-in `curl`, not with
  a real download. The prebuilt CUDA binaries and `LLAMA_CUDA=13.4` were not run.
  Making a start state for `LLM_LONG_START` by continuing a validation run's
  `fixed-pimple` was not tried with this code; the runs here used the developed
  state of October 22.
- `PODFoam_Debug`, `APMOSFoam` and `AEFoam` keep the same patterns (the first two
  `array[:,0].reshape(-1,1)`, the third `np.expand_dims`, all a plain
  `pEqn.solve()`). They were read, not run.
- Wrong-setup test: one case, five wrong runs, and the normal runs are pieces of
  one trajectory, so other normal runs might lie outside its ranges.
